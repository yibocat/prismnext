# Pattern: Contribution-first Introduction

> Reference pattern, not a mold — adapt, blend, reorder, or depart as the
> material demands. The bar is a section that reads true, not one that
> matches this file.

Best when the audience already knows the problem (a methods community, a
journal readership) and the news is *what is now possible*. Weakness: reads
as a claim list if the positioning paragraph is skipped.

1. **The headline** — one or two sentences: what this document establishes.
   No wind-up; the result or capability itself.
2. **The contributions** — the claim list, up front: one checkable claim per
   item, each mapped to its delivering section. Quantify where receipts
   exist ("reduces X by Y% under Z — Section 4"), never decorate.
3. **Positioning** — now situate: the line of work each claim extends or
   overturns, with grounded citations. Keep to what the claims actually
   touch — broad synthesis belongs to Related Work.
4. **Why this was hard / why now** — the obstacle that made previous
   attempts stop short, and what changed.
5. **Roadmap** — one line per section.

Check before done: the headline survives without adjectives; each claim in
item 2 names evidence or its delivering section; positioning cites only
what the claims touch.
