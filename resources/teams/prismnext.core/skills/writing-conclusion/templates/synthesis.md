# Pattern: Synthesis Conclusion

> Reference pattern, not a mold — adapt, blend, reorder, or depart as the
> material demands. The bar is a section that reads true, not one that
> matches this file.

The standard close: answers → limitations → implications → future work.

1. **Answers** — one short paragraph per Introduction question: the answer
   at the strength the evidence supports. Quote the headline number once,
   with its receipt anchor.
2. **Limitations** — a paragraph or short list: each limitation, what it
   threatens, and what survives it.
3. **Implications** — what changes for a practitioner / theorist / tool
   builder who believes the results. Concrete, decision-shaped.
4. **Future work** — one paragraph: the two or three next steps this exact
   evidence makes possible.

Check before done: every answer traces to a Results subsection; no
limitation is news to someone who read Results carefully.
