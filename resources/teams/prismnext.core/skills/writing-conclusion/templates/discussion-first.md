# Pattern: Discussion-first Conclusion

> Reference pattern, not a mold — adapt, blend, reorder, or depart as the
> material demands. The bar is a section that reads true, not one that
> matches this file.

Best when the meaning of the results needs argument — mechanism, boundary
conditions, alternative explanations — before any recap.

1. **What the evidence means** — the interpretation argued for: mechanism,
   boundary conditions, why these results hang together (or deliberately
   do not).
2. **Alternative explanations** — the strongest rival readings and why the
   evidence favors yours (or where you cannot separate them — say so).
3. **Then the recap** — one tight paragraph answering the Introduction's
   questions, now that the interpretation is on the table.
4. **Limitations and implications** — as in the synthesis template.
5. **Future work** — one paragraph, concrete.

Check before done: the interpretation is argued from Results evidence
(every step has a receipt); rival explanations are stated fairly enough
that their proponents would sign them.
