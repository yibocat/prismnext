# Pattern: Story-arc Introduction

> Reference pattern, not a mold — adapt, blend, reorder, or depart as the
> material demands. The bar is a section that reads true, not one that
> matches this file.

Best when the contribution needs context to appreciate — an emerging
phenomenon, a shift in assumptions, a contradiction in received wisdom.
Weakness: the slowest to the point; the reader must trust you for a page.
Keep the arc tight.

1. **The received picture** — what the field believed, with citations. Tell
   it fairly enough that its proponents would sign it.
2. **The crack** — the observation, result, or regime change that the
   received picture cannot absorb. This is usually your early evidence or a
   grounded observation from the literature.
3. **The reframe** — the question restated so the crack becomes expected.
   This is the paper's intellectual move; one paragraph, no results yet.
4. **What we do** — contributions as checkable claims under the reframe,
   each mapped to its delivering section.
5. **Roadmap** — one line per section.

Check before done: the received picture is fairly stated (no strawman);
the crack is grounded (citation or receipt); the reframe is stated as a
claim, not a vibe.
