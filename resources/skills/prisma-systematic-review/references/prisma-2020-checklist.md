# PRISMA 2020 Checklist (condensed)

The 27 items, compressed for working use. Full wording: prisma-statement.org.

## Title & Abstract
1. **Title** — identify as a systematic review.
2. **Abstract** — structured summary (background, methods, results, conclusions).

## Introduction
3. **Rationale** — why this review, in the context of existing knowledge.
4. **Objectives** — explicit question(s) (PICO/SPIDER framing).

## Methods
5. **Eligibility criteria** — inclusion/exclusion, and how grouped for synthesis.
6. **Information sources** — all databases/registers, with coverage dates.
7. **Search strategy** — full search strings as executed, per source, with dates.
8. **Selection process** — who/what screened, how many reviewers, consensus method.
9. **Data collection process** — extraction method, verification.
10. **Data items** — variables sought; assumptions made when unclear.
11. **Risk of bias** — tools used per study (e.g. RoB 2, ROBINS-I).
12. **Effect measures** — effect sizes / measures used in synthesis.
13. **Synthesis methods** — grouping, heterogeneity, meta-analysis model if any.
14. **Reporting bias assessment** — publication bias methods (funnel, tests).
15. **Certainty assessment** — GRADE or equivalent.

## Results
16. **Study selection** — the flow diagram counts (identify/screen/include) with exclusion reasons.
17. **Study characteristics** — table of included studies.
18. **Risk of bias in studies** — per-study results.
19. **Results of individual studies** — per-study effect data.
20. **Results of syntheses** — pooled results, heterogeneity.
21. **Reporting biases** — assessment results.
22. **Certainty of evidence** — per outcome.

## Discussion
23. **Interpretation** — in context of other evidence.
24. **Limitations** — of the evidence AND of the review process.
25. **Implications** — for practice, policy, future research.

## Other
26. **Registration & protocol** — where registered; protocol access; amendments.
27. **Support / competing interests / data availability** — funding, conflicts,
    and where the screening log & data live.
