# Pattern: Question-first Results

> Reference pattern, not a mold — adapt, blend, reorder, or depart as the
> material demands. The bar is a section that reads true, not one that
> matches this file.

Best for exploratory or multi-question studies — one subsection per
research question.

1. **Question restated** — from the outline / brief.
2. **The experiment that answers it** — design in two or three sentences
   (full protocol lives in Methods); why this design can answer the
   question.
3. **The answer** — numbers with full reporting blocks; figures with
   "what to look at" sentences. The answer may be "no effect" — that is an
   answer.
4. **Caveats and follow-ups** — what this answer does *not* establish;
   which later question picks up the thread.

End the section with a short synthesis paragraph: the questions, their
answers, and the combined picture in one table or paragraph.

Check before done: every question from the outline has exactly one
subsection; the synthesis matches the subsections (no new claims appear in
the summary).
